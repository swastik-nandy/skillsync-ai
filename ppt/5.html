RAG Pipeline Explained

Sequential 6-step semantic processing and retrieval workflow.

1

Document Chunking

Resume split into ~120-word chunks

2

Vector Generation

Chunks embedded into 384-dim vectors

3

Vector Indexing

FAISS IndexFlatL2 indexes embeddings (GPU-accelerated)

4

Query Embedding

Job description embedded and searched against resume

5

Semantic Retrieval

Top-5 most relevant resume sections retrieved

6

LLM Analysis

Retrieved context sent to Gemini for analysis
